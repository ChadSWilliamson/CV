CertWatch

SSL & Domain Expiry Monitor

Multi-tenant SaaS · live in production

A monitoring platform built solo so businesses stop finding out their certificate or domain expired from an angry customer. It checks real customer domains daily, deduplicates alerts, and emails you before anything breaks.

  • SSL certificate expiry monitoring
  • Domain expiry via RDAP, with WHOIS fallback
  • Certificate Transparency log monitoring (crt.sh)
  • DNS record change detection
  • Subdomain takeover detection (dangling CNAME checks)
  • Uptime monitoring, checked every 15 minutes
  • Email alerts via Resend, with alert deduplication
